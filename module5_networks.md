# Module 5 – Computer Networks

## What is a Network?

A **computer network** is a group of computers connected together so they can share:
- Information
- Files
- Internet access
- Printers
- Resources

## Types of Networks

### 1. LAN (Local Area Network)
- Small area
- Home, school, office
- Fast and secure

### 2. WAN (Wide Area Network)
- Large distances
- Connects cities or countries
- The internet is the biggest WAN

### 3. Wi‑Fi Networks
- Wireless connection
- Uses radio waves
- Common in homes and public places

## Network Devices

### Router
- Connects your home to the internet
- Sends data to the right device

### Modem
- Converts internet signals from your provider

### Switch
- Connects multiple devices inside a network

### Access Point
- Provides Wi‑Fi coverage

## How Data Moves
Data travels in small pieces called **packets**.  
Packets move through:
- Cables  
- Wi‑Fi signals  
- Routers  
- Servers  

## Why Networks Matter
Networks allow you to:
- Browse the internet  
- Play online games  
- Share files  
- Use cloud services  
- Connect with people worldwide
