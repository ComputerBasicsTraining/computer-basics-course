# Module 10 – Digital Citizenship

## What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is **responsible and safe behavior online**.  
It includes how you communicate, protect yourself, and interact with others.

## Key Areas of Digital Citizenship

### 1. Online Safety
- Protect personal information
- Use strong passwords
- Avoid suspicious websites

### 2. Respectful Communication
- Be polite online
- Avoid bullying or harassment
- Think before posting

### 3. Privacy Awareness
- Check privacy settings
- Understand what apps collect
- Share only what is necessary

### 4. Digital Footprint
- Everything you post can stay online
- Employers may check your online presence
- Be mindful of long‑term impact

### 5. Responsible Use
- Follow rules and laws
- Don’t pirate software or media
- Use technology ethically

## Good Digital Habits
- Log out of accounts
- Update software
- Use secure Wi‑Fi
- Back up important files
